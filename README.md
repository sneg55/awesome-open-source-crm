# Awesome Open Source CRM [![Awesome](https://awesome.re/badge.svg)](https://awesome.re)

> Self-hostable open source software for managing customer and personal relationships.

Every entry is openly licensed, maintained and self-hostable. Entries are listed alphabetically within each section. See the [inclusion criteria](CONTRIBUTING.md#inclusion-criteria).

## Personal CRM

- [Contrack](https://github.com/arvarik/contrack) - Contact manager that imports, deduplicates and enriches contacts, with follow-up reminders and private address books for each family or team member.
- [Meerkat CRM](https://github.com/meerkatcrm/meerkat-crm) - Digital rolodex with contact circles, relationships, employment history and birthday reminders.
- [Monica](https://github.com/monicahq/monica) - Personal relationship manager for keeping track of friends, family and business contacts.
- [Nametag](https://github.com/mattogodoy/nametag) - Personal relationship manager that tracks birthdays and contact details and maps how people are connected.
- [PingCRM](https://github.com/sneg55/pingcrm) - Networking CRM that syncs Gmail, Telegram, X and LinkedIn, scores relationships and drafts follow-ups.
- [Tilly](https://github.com/carlassmann/tilly) - Relationship journal built as an offline-capable web app with client-side sync.

## Business CRM

- [Atomic CRM](https://github.com/marmelab/atomic-crm) - Contacts, tasks, notes and a Kanban deal pipeline, built with React, shadcn/ui and Supabase for customization.
- [BottleCRM](https://github.com/Django-CRM/Django-CRM) - Sales, support tickets and invoicing in one application.
- [Comp AI CRM](https://github.com/trycompai/crm) - CRM operated by an AI agent that researches contacts, schedules its own follow-ups and records only sourced facts.
- [Customermates](https://github.com/customermates/customermates) - Client CRM with an AGPL core, MCP and REST APIs and webhooks, deployed with Docker Compose.
- [DaybydayCRM](https://github.com/Bottelet/DaybydayCRM) - Leads, tasks and clients organized around a team's daily workflow.
- [Deskcomm CRM](https://github.com/melgarafael/DeskcommCRM) - Sales CRM for WhatsApp with AI agents that answer and qualify leads.
- [Django CRM](https://github.com/DjangoCRM/django-crm) - Leads, deals, tasks, projects and email campaigns, built on Django.
- [EspoCRM](https://github.com/espocrm/espocrm) - CRM application with a single-page frontend and a REST API.
- [Fat Free CRM](https://github.com/fatfreecrm/fat_free_crm) - Ruby on Rails CRM for contacts, leads, accounts and opportunities.
- [Frappe CRM](https://github.com/frappe/crm) - Sales CRM for leads and deals, built on the Frappe framework.
- [Krayin](https://github.com/krayin/laravel-crm) - Customer, lead and sales management built with Laravel.
- [NextCRM](https://github.com/pdovhomilja/nextcrm-app) - CRM with projects, invoicing and documents, built with Next.js.
- [OroCRM](https://github.com/oroinc/crm-application) - CRM for marketing, sales and customer service, built on Symfony.
- [Relaticle](https://github.com/relaticle/relaticle) - Companies, people, opportunities, tasks and notes, with a REST API that AI agents can read and update.
- [SuiteCRM](https://github.com/SuiteCRM/SuiteCRM) - CRM for sales, marketing and customer service, forked from SugarCRM Community Edition.
- [tiny-crm](https://github.com/frikishaan/tiny-crm) - Small CRM built with Filament PHP.
- [Twenty](https://github.com/twentyhq/twenty) - CRM with a customizable data model, built with TypeScript, NestJS and React.
- [Warpdrive](https://github.com/sneg55/warpdrive) - Business development CRM modeled on Pipedrive, with pipelines, a deal workspace and two-way Gmail sync.

## ERP Suites with CRM

- [Dolibarr](https://github.com/Dolibarr/dolibarr) - Modular ERP and CRM for small businesses and foundations.
- [ERPNext](https://github.com/frappe/erpnext) - ERP with CRM, sales, accounting, inventory and manufacturing modules.
- [Ever Gauzy](https://github.com/ever-co/ever-gauzy) - Business management platform covering ERP, CRM, HR, applicant tracking and projects.
- [IDURAR](https://github.com/idurar/idurar-erp-crm) - ERP and CRM with accounting and invoicing, built with Node.js and React.
- [koalixcrm](https://github.com/KoalixSwitzerland/koalixcrm) - CRM and ERP built on Django.
- [Odoo](https://github.com/odoo/odoo) - Suite of business applications that includes CRM, sales, accounting and inventory.

## Nonprofit and Community CRM

- [ChurchCRM](https://github.com/ChurchCRM/CRM) - Church management for people, families, groups, events, attendance and giving.
- [CiviCRM](https://github.com/civicrm/civicrm-core) - Constituent relationship management for nonprofits, covering contacts, donations, memberships, events and email.

## CRM Frameworks

- [Corteza](https://github.com/cortezaproject/corteza) - Low-code platform for building CRM and other record-based applications.
- [Laravel CRM](https://github.com/venturedrake/laravel-crm) - Laravel package that adds CRM features to an application or runs as a standalone CRM.
- [NocoBase](https://github.com/nocobase/nocobase) - Low-code platform for building business systems such as CRMs, with an Apache-2.0 core.
